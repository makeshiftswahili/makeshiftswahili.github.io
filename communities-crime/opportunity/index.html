Compare street networks

The same road hierarchy is shown for both neighborhoods. Zoom and pan to inspect the structure within each neighborhood and the surrounding area.

Look for: dense versus sparse local street networks, major arterials cutting through neighborhoods, freeway barriers, multiple versus limited routes in and out, and differences in connectivity between the two neighborhoods.

Interpret the hierarchy: local streets organize movement within neighborhoods, while larger roads can increase accessibility and traffic but may also divide residential areas or channel activity into particular corridors.
