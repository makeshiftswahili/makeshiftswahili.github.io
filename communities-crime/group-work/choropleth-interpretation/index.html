Interpreting Choropleth Maps

Describe what the map shows before you try to explain why the pattern exists. This activity practices the map-reading skills you will use throughout the comparative neighborhood assignments.

The basic routine

Four things to look for

1. LevelAre values generally high, low, or mixed?
2. PatternAre similar values clustered, dispersed, concentrated in a pocket, or arranged in a gradient?
3. LocationWhere in the neighborhood is the pattern occurring?
4. ComparisonHow do the neighborhoods differ in both overall level and internal spatial pattern?
