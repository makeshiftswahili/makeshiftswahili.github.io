Create your five maps

Click any block group to inspect exact group shares before interpreting boundaries.

Map controls: Use the + and − buttons to zoom. For finer-grained zooming, click the map, hold Ctrl, and use your mouse wheel. Drag to reposition the map.

Racial-ethnic categories: Each block group is colored by the largest of four groups—White, Latino, Black, or Asian. Other/Multiracial residents are included in the popup percentages but are not used as a fifth map category.

Boundary figures: Use Draw sharp for solid orange boundaries and Draw fuzzy for black zig-zag boundaries. Click to add vertices and double-click to finish. Not every neighborhood must contain either type of boundary.
