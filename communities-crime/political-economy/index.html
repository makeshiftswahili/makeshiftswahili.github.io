Create your four assignment maps

Compare pooled 2012–2016 eviction rates at the block-group level and mortgage investment at the tract level. The mortgage measure is the total number of home-purchase mortgages originated from 2018–2021 per 1,000 owner-occupied housing units in 2020. Click any geography for details.

Map controls: Use the + and − buttons to zoom. For finer control, hold Ctrl (Windows/Linux) or ⌘ Command (macOS) while scrolling. On a trackpad, you can also pinch to zoom. Drag the map to reposition it.

Eviction-rate maps: the rate pools eviction activity across 2012–2016 to reduce the instability of a single-year measure. Zero is shown separately from positive eviction rates. Click a block group to see its percent renter and use that information when interpreting zero or very low eviction rates.

Mortgage-investment maps: the measure follows the count-based logic used by Velez, Lyons, and Boursaw (2012), adapted to a four-year window: total 2018–2021 home-purchase mortgage originations per 1,000 owner-occupied housing units in 2020. Tracts are divided into city-specific quintiles labeled Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, and Very High. These are relative positions within your city, not absolute thresholds, and the same class breaks are used for both neighborhoods. Tracts shown with black diagonal hatching have 0 owner-occupied housing units in 2020, so a mortgage-investment rate cannot be calculated.
