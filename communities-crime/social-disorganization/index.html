Create your six maps

Each variable is mapped for both neighborhoods using the same class breaks and legend. Frame each map carefully before building the Word document.

Map controls: Use the + and − buttons to zoom in and out. For finer-grained zooming, click the map, hold Ctrl, and use your mouse wheel. Drag the map to reposition it.

Satellite imagery is for exploration: Toggle to Satellite to inspect the built environment. Your PNGs and Word document will always export the Street map basemap at the same zoom and position you selected.

Build your assignment document

The Word file follows the Social Disorganization template, fills in your city and neighborhood names, inserts all six maps and captions, and preserves the red writing prompts.

Maps must finish loading first.

Before submitting: replace the prompts with your own writing and delete all red/bracketed prompt text. Refer to the Example Assignment and assigned readings as directed in the template.
