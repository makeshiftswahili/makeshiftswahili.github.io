3

Create your six maps

Each variable is mapped for both neighborhoods using the same class breaks and legend. Frame each map carefully before building the Word document.

Map controls: Use the + and − buttons to zoom. For finer control, hold Ctrl (Windows/Linux) or ⌘ Command (macOS) while scrolling. On a trackpad, you can also pinch to zoom. Drag the map to reposition it.

Satellite imagery is for exploration: Toggle to Satellite to inspect the built environment. Your Word document will always use the Street map basemap at the same zoom and position you selected.

Legend preview: Use Legend preview to show the legend at the same size and position it will occupy in the Word figure. Use the − and + buttons to choose among a few small legend sizes. Hiding the preview does not remove the legend from the Word document.

4

Build your assignment document

The Word file follows the Social Disorganization template, fills in your city and neighborhood names, inserts all six maps and captions, preserves the red writing prompts, and leaves a blank line for your LSU ID.

Maps must finish loading first.

Before submitting: enter your LSU ID on the blank line in the Word document, replace the prompts with your own writing, and delete all red/bracketed prompt text. Refer to the Example Assignment and assigned readings as directed in the template.
